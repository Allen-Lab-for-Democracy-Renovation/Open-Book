# OpenBook Guide for Department Staff

This guide is for department heads and staff who submit capital expenditure requests (equipment, vehicles, facility repairs, infrastructure projects) through OpenBook's staff portal. It works in any web browser, and no technical knowledge is needed.

The staff portal is internal. Your requests, their amounts, and the finance office's notes and decisions are visible only to you and the finance office, never to the public.

## Account setup

You need an invite link from your finance office; you can't create an account without one. Ask them for one if you don't have it.

1. Open the invite link. It looks like `https://budget.yourtown.gov/staff/join?token=...`. The link is tied to your email address and works only once, so don't share it.
2. Your email is filled in already. Enter your **full name**, a **password** (at least 6 characters), and your **department** (e.g. Public Works).
3. Click **Create Account**. You're signed in and taken to your dashboard.

---

## Signing in

Go to `https://budget.yourtown.gov/staff/login` (use your town's address) and enter your email and password. Bookmark the page; the public budget portal doesn't link to it.

Forgot your password? Ask your finance office for a reset link. Opening it lets you choose a new password.

## Submitting a request

Click **Submit New Capital Request** on your dashboard and fill in the form:

| Field | Required | What to enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Department** | Yes | Your department, e.g. `Public Works`. |
| **Purpose** | Yes | A short, specific title. This is the label the finance office sees in its list, e.g. `HVAC Replacement — Town Hall Second Floor`. |
| **Description** | No | What's being purchased or done, with any specifications or background. |
| **Amount ($)** | Yes | Numbers only, without `$` or commas, e.g. `350000`. |
| **Funding Source** | No | If you have one in mind, e.g. `Free Cash`, `Chapter 90`, `Grant`. Leave it blank if you're unsure; the finance office can decide. |
| **Justification** | Yes | Why it's needed, what problem it solves, and the risk of deferring it. |
| **Fiscal Year** | Yes | The year you're requesting funding for, e.g. `FY2027`. Match how your town labels fiscal years. |

A strong justification is specific, for example: *"HVAC units are original to the 1998 construction. Two failed last winter, requiring $18,000 in emergency repairs. Continued operation risks closing the building during heating season."*

Click **Submit Request**. Check your entries first: you can't edit a request once it's submitted.

## Request status

Your dashboard shows how many requests you've submitted, how many are pending, how many are approved, and your five most recent requests. Click **History** in the top bar to see all of them, filter by status, and click a request to open its details and any notes from the finance office.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Submitted** | Received and waiting for review. |
| **Under Review** | The finance office is reviewing it. |
| **Recommended** | The finance office is putting it forward (e.g. to the Select Board or Town Meeting), but there's no final decision yet. |
| **Approved** | The finance office has approved it. |
| **Denied** | Not approved for this cycle. |

"Approved" means the finance office has accepted the request for consideration. It doesn't appropriate funds; that still happens through your town's normal process, such as a Town Meeting vote.

## Frequently asked questions

**I made a mistake in a request.** Submitted requests can't be edited or deleted. Submit a corrected request, and tell your finance office which one replaces the old one.

**My invite link says it's invalid or already used.** Each link works once. If you've already created your account, sign in at `/staff/login` instead. Otherwise, ask your finance office for a new invite.

**Can I submit for another department?** The form accepts any department name, but submit only for your own. The finance office can see who submitted each request.
