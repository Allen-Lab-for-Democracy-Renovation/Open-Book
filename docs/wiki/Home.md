# OpenBook Wiki

Welcome to the OpenBook documentation. OpenBook is a free, open-source budget transparency portal for municipalities. This wiki contains step-by-step guides for everyone involved in setting up and using OpenBook.

![The public portal, showing the banner, headline figures, and sections for expenses, revenues, and capital](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Allen-Lab-for-Democracy-Renovation/Open-Book/main/docs/screenshots/portal-overview.png)

---

## Choose Your Guide

### I am a Town Manager, Finance Director, or Select Board Member
→ [Guide for Town Managers](Guide-for-Town-Managers)

Learn what OpenBook is, what it costs, what your team needs to do, and how to get started — no technical knowledge required.

### I am an IT staff member setting up the hosting
→ [Guide for IT Departments](Guide-for-IT-Departments)

Step-by-step instructions for deploying OpenBook to a server or cloud hosting platform, setting up the database, connecting a domain, and handing off to finance staff.

### I am finance or admin staff managing the portal day-to-day
→ [Guide for Finance Staff](Guide-for-Finance-Staff)

How to use the admin panel: uploading budget data, adding tooltips and explanations, managing documents and links, and reviewing resident questions and capital requests.

---

### I am a department head or town employee submitting capital requests
→ [Guide for Department Staff](Guide-for-Department-Staff)

How to register for the staff portal, submit capital expenditure requests, and track their status.

## About OpenBook

OpenBook is maintained by the Allen Lab for Democracy Renovation. The source code is available at [github.com/Allen-Lab-for-Democracy-Renovation/Open-Book](https://github.com/Allen-Lab-for-Democracy-Renovation/Open-Book).

Questions or issues can be reported on the [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/Allen-Lab-for-Democracy-Renovation/Open-Book/issues).
